How each number is counted

Visit: a signed-in member opening This Week or the Archive. Repeat page loads within 30 minutes count as one visit.
Active: at least one visit, read-paper click, like or comment in that period.
Returning: active in this period and the one before it.
Read-paper click: a click on a card's "Read paper" link.
Likes and comments: read from the tables CarcinoS already keeps, so history from before tracking started is included.
Emailed to: from the existing digest delivery records. Signed-out visitors are not tracked. Times are Eastern.
