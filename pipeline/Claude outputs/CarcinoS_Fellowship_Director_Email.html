Dear Fellowship Program Director,
I'm writing to introduce CarcinoS — a free, curated oncology evidence platform I hope you'll consider sharing with your fellows. Oncology fellows carry an extraordinary cognitive load: mastering protocols, managing complex patients, and simultaneously keeping pace with a literature that grows by hundreds of relevant publications each year. With physician burnout rates at all-time highs, tools that meaningfully reduce this burden without sacrificing rigor are more important than ever.
CarcinoS monitors top journals, major conferences, and regulatory updates, then surfaces and scores the studies most likely to impact patient care — delivered as a weekly digest every Tuesday evening. It is designed by oncologists, for oncologists, and carries none of the noise of a general medical news feed.
•  Weekly digest — practice-changing papers only, filtered by disease site, every Tuesday
•  Platform access — Incremental & Horizon views of the literature, browsable by week and disease site
•  Searchable archive — everything CarcinoS has surfaced, growing every week
•  Built for oncology — designed by oncologists for the practicing clinician, not a general medical feed
