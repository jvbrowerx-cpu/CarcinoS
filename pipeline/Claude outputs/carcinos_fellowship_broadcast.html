For your hematology/oncology fellows
Hello — we're reaching out to let you know about a free oncology literature resource we think your fellows will find valuable. CarcinoS monitors the oncology literature in the background so they don't have to—surfacing the most clinically relevant findings for the disease sites they follow, and alerting them when something worth knowing is published.

No searching, no noise. When there's a practice-impacting update, fellows receive a concise digest Tuesday evening. If there's nothing worth sharing that week, they won't hear from us. CarcinoS is free, recently launched, and growing quickly across oncology programs in the US and abroad — we'd love to help your fellows stay up to date.
• Weekly email digest filtered to each fellow's disease sites — practice-changing papers only
• Incremental & Horizon views of the literature, browsable by week and disease site
• Searchable archive of curated papers, organized by disease site and expanding every week
• Comment threads on articles to discuss findings with colleagues NEW
Free — takes about a minute to set up
