**Subject:** A free oncology literature tool for your fellows — CarcinoS

Hi [First Name],

I'm writing to introduce CarcinoS (carcinos.app) — a free, weekly oncology literature digest built specifically for oncology fellows and attendings.

Each Tuesday, fellows who subscribe get a curated digest of that week's highest-impact oncology publications, organized by disease site, ranked by clinical significance, and labeled "Practice Impacting" so they can stay current without wading through PubMed. This week we're also highlighting key abstracts from ASTRO 2026.

We now have nearly 150 subscribers and are reaching out to fellowship programs to let fellows know it exists. I'd be grateful if you'd be willing to:
- Forward this note to your fellows, or

Subscribing takes under 30 seconds at carcinos.app — no cost, no commitment.

Happy to answer any questions. And if Dr. [Program Director Last Name] would find it useful, I'd love to add them to the list too.

Thanks for what you do for your program,

## Notes on Personalization

- Replace [First Name] / Dr. [Last Name] with the contact's actual name
- For programs with a generic inbox (e.g., hemoncfellowship@vumc.org), use Version A and address it to "Fellowship Team" or "Hi there"
- Consider adding 1 sentence of disease-site relevance for large programs (e.g., for MSK: "We cover solid tumor and heme malignancies extensively, including GYN and GU oncology")
- Best send time: Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am local time
- Follow-up: if no response in 10–14 days, one gentle follow-up is appropriate
